# Getting Started with FCRO

Welcome to the Frontlines Combat Realism Overhaul! This guide will help you get started with FCRO and understand its key changes.

## Installation

### Requirements

- **Gates of Hell: Ostfront** (base game)
- **Their Finest Hour** DLC (recommended for full content)
- Compatible with most other mods (check mod compatibility notes)

### Installation Steps

1. **Subscribe to FCRO** on Steam Workshop or download from your mod source
2. **Enable the mod** in Gates of Hell launcher
3. **Start a new game** or mission to see changes take effect
   - Existing saves may work but could have unexpected behavior
4. **Check mod load order** if using multiple mods
   - FCRO should generally load after balance mods
   - Load before cosmetic/sound mods for best compatibility

### Verification

After installation, verify FCRO is working:

1. Start a skirmish or test mission
2. Check infantry rifle ranges (should be 180m for bolt-action rifles)
3. Check tank gun ranges (should show +25% from vanilla)
4. Look for improved accuracy at range

## What's Different?

FCRO makes comprehensive changes to Gates of Hell. Here are the most important differences:

### Extended Engagement Ranges

All weapons receive 20-50% range increases:

- **Infantry rifles**: 120m → 180m (+50%)
- **Machine guns**: 120m → 140m (+17%)
- **Tank guns**: +25% across the board
- **Mortars**: 140-230m → 175-290m (+25%)
- **Anti-tank weapons**: +25-40% depending on type

**Impact**: Combat happens at more realistic distances. You'll engage enemies further out, and positioning becomes more important.

### Increased Lethality

Damage values are significantly increased:

- **Rifle damage**: 70 → 110 (+57%)
- **Machine gun damage**: 80 → 110 (+38%)
- **Armor penetration**: More lethal crew kills on penetration
- **Explosives**: Grenades and mines have extended lethal radii

**Impact**: Combat is faster and more decisive. Units die quicker, making cover and tactics essential.

### Improved Accuracy Systems

Better accuracy curves and AI behavior:

- Tighter accuracy at effective ranges
- Realistic degradation beyond effective range
- Smarter AI firing timeouts
- Veterancy provides accuracy bonuses instead of just health

**Impact**: Experienced units are deadly accurate. Player skill and unit positioning matter more than raw numbers.

### Reduced Spotting

Detection ranges reduced by ~20%:

- Harder to spot stationary infantry
- Ambushes more effective
- Cover and concealment matter significantly
- Silenced weapons have zero detection

**Impact**: Stealth and positioning are rewarded. You can set up ambushes and use terrain effectively.

## Gameplay Tips for FCRO

### Infantry Combat

1. **Use cover religiously** - The lethality increase makes cover essential
2. **Go prone when possible** - Provides massive accuracy and concealment bonuses
3. **Space out your units** - Extended ranges mean explosives affect larger areas
4. **Flank, don't charge** - Direct assaults are suicide with increased lethality
5. **Use veteran units carefully** - They're much more effective but still mortal

### Tank Combat

1. **Engage at range** - Your guns reach further, use it
2. **Hull-down positions** - Even more important with increased penetration
3. **Support your armor** - Infantry screens are critical
4. **Know your ammunition** - APCR for heavy armor, APHE for lighter targets
5. **Crew matters** - Penetration kills crew more reliably; protect your veterans

### Artillery & Support

1. **Mortars have extended range** - Use them from safer positions
2. **Pre-registered fires** - Set up likely ambush zones
3. **Rocket artillery saturation** - Extended ranges make these devastating
4. **Smoke is crucial** - Reduced spotting makes smoke screens more effective

### Advanced Tactics

1. **Reverse slope defense** - Hide behind ridges, pop up to fire
2. **Ambush corridors** - Reduced spotting allows close-range ambushes
3. **Fire and maneuver** - One unit suppresses while another flanks
4. **Overwatch positions** - Put snipers and MGs in elevated positions
5. **Combined arms** - Infantry, armor, and artillery work together

## Common Questions

### Will this work with my existing saves?

Maybe, but not recommended. Start a new game for best results.

### Can I use this with other mods?

Generally yes, but:
- Mods that change weapon stats may conflict
- Visual/sound mods should be compatible
- Check load order: FCRO should load after other balance mods

### Is this historically accurate?

FCRO aims for practical realism within game constraints. Ranges and damage reflect historical capabilities scaled to the game's 8:1 scale (180m in-game ≈ 1440m real-world).

### Why does my infantry die so fast?

This is intentional. FCRO increases lethality to match real combat. Use cover, spacing, and tactics.

### Why can't I spot enemies as easily?

Spotting ranges are reduced 20% to reward concealment and positioning. Use recon units and careful scouting.

### What's the best faction?

All factions are balanced but with historical flavor:
- **Germans**: Best optics and accuracy
- **Soviets**: High-damage HE shells, numerous
- **Americans**: Good all-around, premium ammo
- **British**: APDS rounds for penetration
